01 — About Logicraft

Built on experience.
Moving beyond boundaries.

Logicraft Global FZCO is a Dubai-based shipping and logistics company, incorporated in 2025. We specialise in international breakbulk and dry bulk cargo transportation, with established expertise in log shipments and a growing portfolio across a wider range of commodities.

Through Time Charter and Voyage Charter solutions, we coordinate tailored ocean freight movements for cargo owners, traders and end users. Our in-house cargo programmes and operational focus support dependable execution from origin to discharge.
